## Method

- **Data.** BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, 1d bars through `load_klines` (sealed), cut at 2025-01-01: no bar of the validation year or the vault enters.
- **Models are per pair.** Each pair's frame, folds and fits use that pair's rows alone; BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT are never pooled.
- **Walk-forward.** Point-in-time features, three-class labels with h = 1, one refit per month over the test months 2019-01 -> 2024-12, expanding window, purge and embargo h. Rows before the window only train.
  - BTCUSDT: 72 folds, 470-2631 training rows; scored bars 2019-01-01 -> 2024-12-31 (2192 bars, 2191 daily returns), the same for every strategy and venue.
  - ETHUSDT: 72 folds, 470-2631 training rows; scored bars 2019-01-01 -> 2024-12-31 (2192 bars, 2191 daily returns), the same for every strategy and venue.
- **Execution.** A target decided at a bar's close executes at the next open, at the taker rate, with 2 bps of slippage per side and Binance's order filters (BTCUSDT: min notional 5, step 1e-05; ETHUSDT: min notional 5, step 0.0001). Initial cash 10,000 USDT. The first scored bar never trades, the last target never executes, and an open position is marked at the last close without an exit fee.
- **Forecasters to targets.** climatology, ridge and xgboost go long when the expected return clears the venue's round trip, `2 * (taker + 2 bps)`, and back to flat when it turns negative (hysteresis). Their targets therefore depend on the venue.
- **Statistics.** Sharpe ratios are annualized with sqrt(365) from daily simple returns, no risk-free rate. Analytic interval: Mertens (2002), iid. Bootstrap interval: stationary bootstrap (Politis and Romano 1994), Politis-White block length, 2000 draws, percentile; the block length reads the serial correlation of the returns, not of their squares. Sensitivity, for every paired interval of H9 and of the headline: the mean block max(PW(d), PW((d - mean d)^2)), with d the paired return difference, so that volatility clustering also sets the block. The registered analysis stays the primary. Diebold-Mariano: registered at h = 1 with no lag; sensitivity with a Newey-West (Bartlett) variance and floor(4 (T/100)^(2/9)) lags. PSR(0) and DSR after Bailey and Lopez de Prado (2012, 2014); for the DSR, N is the number of candidates with a defined Sharpe at that pair and venue and V the variance of their per-period Sharpes.
- **random-turnover.** 100 seeds. Its line gives the median and the 5th and 95th percentiles over the seeds; its intervals, PSR, DSR and H9 comparison use the seed with the median Sharpe at that pair and venue (named in the tables).
- **Assumptions.** Today's fee schedules are applied to 2019-2024, a stated assumption, not a leak. Prices are Binance's USDT prices; Bitvavo, Coinbase and Kraken quote BTC and ETH in EUR or USD.

- Ridge cross-check, BTCUSDT: on all 72 folds the numpy ridge equals `olsprime_rs.ridge_fit` on the standardized features; worst max abs difference 4.24e-16 on the coefficients and 4.61e-16 on the fitted values (tolerance 1e-09).
- Ridge cross-check, ETHUSDT: on all 72 folds the numpy ridge equals `olsprime_rs.ridge_fit` on the standardized features; worst max abs difference 1.68e-15 on the coefficients and 1.20e-15 on the fitted values (tolerance 1e-09).
